windflower - Immersive Experience
2025 / 3 mins

We exist in a paradox of memory: we document every second of our lives, yet our internal recollections are constantly fading, overwritten by the noise of the digital present. Windflower is an immersive audio-visual installation that explores this tension between personal anxiety, fleeting memory, and the relentless gaze of global surveillance.

Experimentation with panoramic, three-projector immersive experience setup of up to 10080x1080 in resolution.

Visual: XXHYZ
Music: windflower - shameless. & viznode
