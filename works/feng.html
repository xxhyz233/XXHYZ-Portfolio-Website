Brief.

An attempt to capture all the fleeting lights and noise of the mundane: a forest of geometric shapes, the phosphorescence and afterimages of closed eyelids, eyes of squids, a dream, a bathroom, digits, a fan, falling, retina, thresholds, corners, limits… fragments of memory the brain subconsiously compensates for.

Process.

Experimentation with Gaussian Splatting, Point Clouds, Live Link Cameras, Cinematography and 3DCG vividly expressing the boundaries between reality and the illusory.

After Effects, Premiere, Cinema 4D, Blender, Unreal Engine 5, Houdini, Motion Capture...
