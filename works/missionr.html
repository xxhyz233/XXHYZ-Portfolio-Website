Porsche Mission R
Modeling / Texturing / Look Development

Inspired by Porsche's Mission R electric concept racer, I fully modeled the Mission R in 3DCG, then re-imagined it in the context of the fictional racing team "PINK GROUP".

Planning on exploring motion design with the model in Cinema 4D and After Effects in the future.

Director, 3D, Post-Processing, Livery Design: XXHYZ
Livery Graphic Design: ariiol

Modeling: Blender, Plasticity
Texturing: Substance Painter
Look Development: Cinema 4D
