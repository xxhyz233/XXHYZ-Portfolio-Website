Evanescence (Trailer)

The trailer is only a part of the film in progress, with completion planned for late 2026.

Evanescence imagines a civilization as a microcosm of human biology: an ecosystem of systems and cells caught in a continuous cycle of growth and destruction. Within this fragile balance, a single action can trigger a chain reaction capable of unraveling the entire world.

The project offers a glimpse into this transient ecosystem as it drives itself toward extinction.

Film: XXHYZ
Score: SeyriN

I use both Cinema 4D and Unreal Engine 5 for look development, choosing the tools that best suit each scene’s atmosphere. Unreal Engine’s offline rendering pipeline lets me explore different visual directions quickly, making experimentation more manageable as an independent 3D filmmaker.
